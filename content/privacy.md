## 2. Who can use the Platform

**2.1** The Platform is for people who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we hold data about a person under 18, we will delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it.

## 5. Verification data

**5.1** We use Verification data only to verify identity, prevent fraud, protect Users and meet legal duties. We do not use it for advertising.

**5.2** Verification documents and videos are stored in a separate, access-restricted system. Only authorised Verification staff can open them, and every access is logged.

**5.3** Images of ID documents are deleted within 90 days after the Verification decision. We keep only the ID type, your legal name, the country, the last 4 characters of the ID number, the date of Verification and the outcome. We keep these for as long as your Account exists and for 6 years after it closes.

**5.4** Your Creation Video is kept while your Account is open and for 24 months after it closes, so that we can resolve authenticity disputes. It is never shown publicly. A short clip from it appears on your profile only if you choose to publish it.

## 7. What other Users can see

**7.1** Your public profile, your Artworks and listings, your public posts and comments, your reviews and your Events are visible to other Users and may be visible to people who are not logged in. Do not post anything you want to keep private.

**7.2** A Buyer's name, delivery address and phone number are given to the Artist for that Order only. The Terms require the Artist to use them only to ship the Order, not to share them, and to delete them within 12 months after delivery unless the law requires the Artist to keep them.

**7.3** An Artist's legal name appears on the Certificate of Authenticity given to the Buyer. It is not shown on the public profile, which shows only the professional name, unless the Artist trades under their legal name.

**7.4** Event organisers see the number of RSVPs, and they see your name only if you choose to share it.

## 9. How we protect personal data

**9.1** We protect personal data in these ways:

- all data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256 or an equivalent standard);
- multi-factor authentication is required for all staff and for all Artist and Gallery Accounts;
- staff can open personal data only to the extent their role requires, and every staff member signs a confidentiality agreement;
- an independent security test is carried out at least once every 12 months; and
- encrypted backups are kept, and restoring them is tested at least once every 6 months.

**9.2** If a personal data breach is likely to put people at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. We will tell affected Users without undue delay what happened, what data is involved and what they should do.

## 10. Automated decisions and scanning

**10.1** Some of our checks are automated. We use automated tools to detect fraud and unusual log-ins, and to scan uploaded images and video for child sexual abuse material and Mature Content. An automated fraud check may restrict or suspend an Account.

**10.2** You may ask for a person to review any decision made only by automated means. Write to [dpo@artspace.example] or use the appeal route in Section 13.8 of the Terms. Anything flagged by a scan is reviewed by a trained person before action is taken.
